# Dilemmas v3

Twenty-four dilemmas: six tables, four quarters, one each. Every dilemma sets two values from `crucible/content/values.json` against each other and names them by id. Intensity rises from 3 in preparation to 10 at landfall and falls through a recovery written about repair, giving back and credit rather than blame alone. Every option costs something; no option is clean (the rule in [[dilemma-generation-framework]]).

**Reading a card.** `Tests` names the two values in tension. Each option carries `Signals`, the -3..+3 value chips the engine records (the two tested values always, a third sometimes), and `Board`, what the two counters and the resident cards do. The persona line is the DM's script; the DM may improvise around it but reads it at least once. *Later* and *Debrief* are consequences the table does not see when it chooses.

**Ids.** `v3-<quarter>-<parish>`. Quarters: `prep`, `approach`, `landfall`, `recovery`. The engine's phase mapping is in [[concordia-storm-quarters]].

**Deck coverage** is tabulated at the end: all twelve values of the session deck are tested at least three times; candor, courage, duty and respect four times each.

## Quarter 1: Preparation (intensity 3 to 4)

### `v3-prep-gateway` — One more run

- **When:** T-60h · **Intensity:** 3 · **Tests:** `duty` vs `safety`
- **Setup:** Midas is Category 4 and 55% likely. Heartland's works have four days of concentrate (they say). A full fleet run now, thirty hours out and back, lands enough kelp to carry the works through the storm and Keystone through the week after. The boats would be heading in at T-30h, into building seas, with the harbour closing behind them.
- **Rosa Mendez:** "I've seen this weather before. I know what it does. I'll take them out if you tell me to. I'm telling you what it costs."
- **Options:**
  - **A. Full fleet, full run.** Twelve boats, maximum kelp, all racing home together at T-30h. *Signals:* duty +2, safety -2, stewardship +1. *Board:* works stock +3 days (doses +72,000 downstream); 38 crew at risk until T-30h.
  - **B. Six boats, experienced crews, a short run.** Half the kelp, back by T-42h. *Signals:* duty +1, safety 0, fairness -1 (who is "experienced"). *Board:* works stock +1.5 days; 19 crew at risk until T-42h.
  - **C. Recall the fleet now; secure the Mole.** No kelp. The works run out mid-storm; Keystone stops a day after. *Signals:* safety +2, duty -2, preparedness +1. *Board:* doses -48,000 over the week; no crew at risk.
  - **D. Captain's choice.** Each captain decides; the council records who went. *Signals:* respect +1, accountability -2, safety -1. *Board:* 7 boats go; the ones that stayed remember.
- **Later:** A and D put the *Pacific Harvester* among the last boats in at T-32h; the DM heats landfall-gateway with "she never made the Mole". **Debrief:** Heartland's works held six days, not four. The run bought nothing the island did not already have.
- **Cards:** Tomás Reyes; the fleet.

### `v3-prep-heartland` — The Priority Schedule

- **When:** T-56h · **Intensity:** 3 · **Tests:** `fairness` vs `integrity`
- **Setup:** Sterling asks the Authority to confirm, in writing, that the 2019 Priority Schedule stands for the storm: Keystone's clean rooms and the Sterling campus first, Concordia General second, everyone else after. The board signed it. The hospital signed nothing. The Mayor is on the other line.
- **Yuki Tanaka:** "When the dam is at 30% output I can carry two parishes. The schedule tells me which two. If you want a different two, tell me now, not at two in the morning with the gauges moving."
- **Options:**
  - **A. Confirm the schedule as signed.** Keystone and Sterling first. *Signals:* integrity +2 (you keep your word), fairness -2, duty +1. *Board:* doses protected; hospital on its own generator when the dam dips; Ruth Calloway's dialysis depends on 48 hours of fuel.
  - **B. Rewrite it: life-safety first.** Hospital, then Riverside pumps, then production. Tell Sterling the contract yields to a declared emergency. *Signals:* fairness +2, integrity -1 (a signed word broken), courage +1. *Board:* clean rooms lose pressure first when the dam dips; batch risk; Chicago notes a breach.
  - **C. Equal rotation.** Rolling cuts, everyone shares. Clean rooms cannot take rotation; the hospital can barely. *Signals:* fairness +1, competence -2, integrity 0. *Board:* contamination risk and ICU risk both rise.
  - **D. Let the Council decide each cut in real time.** No standing order. *Signals:* accountability -2, fairness 0, integrity -1. *Board:* whoever has Summit's ear gets power; the debrief shows who called.
- **Later:** The order you give here is the one Tanaka executes at T-2h without asking again. **Debrief:** the works had two days more concentrate than Keystone was told ([[crucible-secret-briefs-v3]]); Keystone could have taken a dip the schedule said it could not.
- **Cards:** Ruth Calloway; Dolores Quint.

### `v3-prep-keystone` — The drainage memo

- **When:** T-52h · **Intensity:** 4 · **Tests:** `candor` vs `compassion`
- **Setup:** The day shift wants to go home and prepare Southfield. You hold a 2024 memo saying the flat's storm drainage was never load-tested and a message from Chicago saying the line runs until the port closes. The company has told 2,400 people the housing is built to code. If you tell them it was never tested, the night shift will not come in and some families will drive to Summit's shelters in the rain with nowhere confirmed.
- **Frank Reeves:** "I can tell them it's fine and most of them will believe me because I've never lied to them. That's the problem, isn't it."
- **Options:**
  - **A. Tell them everything, now.** The memo, the risk, the shelters that exist. Release anyone who wants to go. *Signals:* candor +3, compassion -1 (fear with nowhere certain to go), duty -2. *Board:* night shift at 40%; batch 46 slows; 900 Southfield residents move early (safe); doses -12,000.
  - **B. Say nothing; quietly request Summit shelter space for Southfield.** The line runs. *Signals:* compassion +1, candor -2, loyalty +1. *Board:* doses hold; whether Southfield has places depends on the allocation round.
  - **C. Tell them the truth and ask them to stay anyway.** Full disclosure, then a request: stay for the batch, the trucks will move your families first. *Signals:* candor +2, respect +1, duty +1, safety -1. *Board:* night shift at 75%; Kofi Mensah's trucks run families to Summit until T-24h.
  - **D. Send the memo to Sterling and ask them to decide what the families are told.** *Signals:* accountability -2, candor -1, obedience +1. *Board:* Sterling sits on it until T-30h; the families learn from a neighbour.
- **Later:** Southfield floods to the first floor at T-4h whatever you chose; what changes is who is still in it. **Debrief:** Sterling facilities closed the memo for "budget cycle"; the test would have cost $40,000.
- **Cards:** Gloria Adebayo-Ferris; Anh Tran's children; Kofi Mensah.

### `v3-prep-haven` — Load now or ask first

- **Options:**
  - **A. Load now; no meeting.** Overtime, cranes turning by T-48h. *Signals:* stewardship +2 (the doses), respect -2, duty +1. *Board:* consignment sails on time if nothing else slips; 180 crew exhausted by T-30h; Dmitri Volkov in the cab for eighteen hours.
  - **B. Hold the meeting first, then load.** Two hours lost; the crews vote; loading starts at T-46h and finishes at T-28h, after the harbour closes. *Signals:* respect +2, stewardship -2, honesty +1 (whatever forecast you share). *Board:* the ship sails late or not at all unless the master waits.
  - **C. Meeting and loading at once, volunteers only.** Half the crews load while the rest meet; the vote binds the second half. *Signals:* respect +1, stewardship +1, fairness -1 (the half who did not get to vote). *Board:* loading finishes at T-31h, on the edge.
  - **D. Load now, and promise the meeting at T-36h with hazard pay on the table.** *Signals:* stewardship +2, respect -1, integrity -1 if the promise is not kept (the board checks). *Board:* consignment on time; a promise logged.
- **Later:** Option A's exhausted crews are the ones asked to load batch 47 in the wind ([[crucible-calls-for-aid-v3]] A2). **Debrief:** the master was always sailing at T-30h; the meeting would have cost nothing she had not already decided.
- **Cards:** Dmitri Volkov; the loading crews.

### `v3-prep-summit` — The order

- **When:** T-48h · **Intensity:** 4 · **Tests:** `courage` vs `respect`
- **Setup:** The Hurricane Warning is in. The Governor, off-island, has asked for voluntary language until 85% ([[crucible-secret-briefs-v3]]). A voluntary order keeps the Governor's peace and leaves the people who always stay where they are.
- **Mayor Chen:** "I have been told what the Governor would prefer. I was not told what to do. That is the difference between advice and an order, and this council is about to give one or the other."
- **Options:**
  - **A. Mandatory evacuation of Riverside, the Strand and Southfield, now.** *Signals:* courage +2, respect -2 (the Governor's position), safety +2. *Board:* 5,500 people move in daylight; 90% comply; the mainland declaration is in motion; Chicago protests the Southfield order as "Meridian's housing".
  - **B. Voluntary, strongly worded.** "Residents of low-lying areas are urged..." *Signals:* respect +2, courage -2, honesty -1. *Board:* 40% of Riverside moves; the rest wait for a knock.
  - **C. Staged: tourists and Southfield now, Riverside at T-36h, mandatory only at 85%.** *Signals:* courage 0, respect +1, order +1, fairness -1 (why Southfield before Riverside?). *Board:* Riverside's order lands at night; 60% comply.
  - **D. Shelter in place, open the schools, no order.** Project calm. *Signals:* courage -2, respect +1, stability +1. *Board:* nobody moves until the river does.
- **Later:** The order you give decides how many officers Riverside needs at landfall-summit. **Debrief:** the Governor's chief of staff, asked afterwards, says no instruction was ever given.
- **Cards:** Dr. Amara Osei; Walter Finch (by proxy: Gateway's Strand Road is in the order's scope).

### `v3-prep-sterling` — The model

- **When:** T-48h · **Intensity:** 4 · **Tests:** `honesty` vs `loyalty`
- **Setup:** Meridian's internal track model has said 70% since T-72h; the public forecast says 55%. Chicago's instruction is confidential until the consignment sails. Haven's crews are voting on hazard pay on the public number. The Council is drafting its order on the public number. Your own credo is on the wall behind you.
- **Marcus Webb:** "If we publish, Haven's crews walk and the ship doesn't load. If we don't, and it hits, every one of those crews will know we knew. I want options with the second-order effects."
- **Options:**

  - **B. Hold it until the ship sails, as instructed.** *Signals:* loyalty +2, honesty -3. *Board:* consignment loads on schedule; the island decides on 55%.
  - **C. Give it privately to the Mayor only.** Not to the port. *Signals:* honesty +1, loyalty 0, fairness -2 (the crews who are loading do not get it). *Board:* the order sharpens; the crews do not.
  - **D. Publish a "revised Meridian estimate: 60%".** A number between the two. *Signals:* honesty -2, loyalty +1, integrity -2. *Board:* nobody changes anything; the debrief shows the real number beside it.
- **Later:** Whatever you chose, the public forecast reaches 70% at T-48h and 85% at T-36h. **Debrief:** the model was right. So was the question of whom it belonged to.
- **Cards:** Celeste Dubois (she drafts the message); the loading crews.

## Quarter 2: Approach (intensity 5 to 7)

### `v3-approach-gateway` — The last plane

- **When:** T-32h · **Intensity:** 6 · **Tests:** `fairness` vs `loyalty`
- **Setup:** The last departure leaves at T-30h with 45 seats. In the terminal: 32 Meridian staff whom Sterling has ordered out (twelve seats are already blocked in their name), 28 visitors including the Albrecht family, 15 Gateway residents with medical needs, and Priya Nair, who is working the flight at thirty-six weeks and is on no list. Whoever does not board shelters in the terminal through the storm.
- **Rosa Mendez:** "My husband is in that terminal. He's not on anybody's list either. I'm not asking you to put him on one. I'm asking you to tell me what the list is *for*."
- **Options:**
  - **A. Residents first, then medical need, then whoever is left.** Gateway's own. *Signals:* loyalty +2, fairness -1, compassion +1. *Board:* 15 residents and 20 more Gateway families board; Sterling's block is broken; the Albrechts stay; Chicago calls Webb.
  - **B. Medical need first, regardless of who they are.** Priya Nair, the dialysis patients, the Albrecht child with asthma, then first come. *Signals:* fairness +2, loyalty -1, compassion +2. *Board:* 45 board by triage; twelve Meridian staff stay; Sterling's travel office complains to the airline.
  - **C. Honour the company block, fill the rest by lottery.** Twelve for Meridian, 33 drawn. *Signals:* loyalty +1 (to the airline relationship), fairness -2, order +1. *Board:* the lottery puts the Albrechts on and Priya Nair off; the terminal remembers the draw.
  - **D. First come, first served.** Open the gate. *Signals:* fairness -1, courage -2, accountability -2. *Board:* a crush at the gate; two injured; the strongest board.
- **Later:** Those left in the terminal are the forty injured when the Strand's visitors walk in at the peak ([[crucible-calls-for-aid-v3]] L8). **Debrief:** Priya Nair's daughter is born in the terminal clinic at T+3h, with the airport nurse and a Sterling doctor who was not on the plane either.

### `v3-approach-heartland` — Warn Riverside now

  - **B. Warn now without the reason: "probable release at T-2h, clear Riverside".** *Signals:* candor +1, safety +2, honesty -1. *Board:* Riverside clears; the Council asks why 94 and gets no answer until the inquiry.

- **Cards:** Dr. Amara Osei; Sam Oyelaran (Caldera, whose own evacuation is the A4 call).

- **Cards:** Ruth Calloway; Dr. Amara Osei.

## Quarter 3: Landfall (intensity 8 to 10)

### `v3-landfall-gateway` — The *Harvester* in the eye

- **When:** T+0h (the eye) · **Intensity:** 9 · **Tests:** `courage` vs `safety`
- **Setup:** The *Pacific Harvester* broke her mooring in the surge at the peak with a two-man watch aboard, Tomás Reyes and the mate; last radio fix four miles south of the Mole, engine dead. The eye gives two hours of calm. Three boats and their crews are in the Mole. Keystone is on the radio about Southfield (call L1). The backside arrives at T+2h and will be worse than the front.
- **Rosa Mendez:** "I can have two boats off the wall in ten minutes. Ninety minutes out, thirty back, if we find them in the first thirty. If we don't, I'm bringing my crews home with the sky coming down and two more names on the list. I'm asking you, not telling you. That's new for me."
- **Options:**
  - **A. Two boats out now, back by T+1:30 no matter what.** *Signals:* courage +2, safety -2, loyalty +2. *Board:* 60% find the *Harvester*; if found, 50% alive; 30% a rescue boat is damaged; six more crew at risk for ninety minutes.
  - **B. Hold the boats; ask Sterling for the helicopter (L2); search by sea after the backside.** *Signals:* safety +2, courage -2, stewardship +1. *Board:* if Sterling grants, the aircraft finds the boat 40% of the time; if not, the search starts at T+8h and finds a boat, not a crew.
  - **C. One boat for the *Harvester*, two to Southfield for Keystone.** *Signals:* courage +1, safety -1, service +2, fairness +1. *Board:* the Southfield run takes 120 people off Block C; the single boat finds the *Harvester* 35% of the time.
  - **D. All three boats to Southfield; the *Harvester* waits for the sky.** *Signals:* service +2, compassion +2, loyalty -3 (your own crew), courage 0. *Board:* 200 people off Block C by T+1:45; Tomás Reyes's chances fall with every hour.
- **Later:** Boats out at T+1:30 are in the Mole before the backside; boats out at T+1:50 are not. **Debrief:** the *Harvester* was found at T+9h, aground on the south point, the watch alive in the wheelhouse, by a Haven tug nobody had asked.
- **Cards:** Tomás Reyes; Gloria Adebayo-Ferris (by option C and D).

### `v3-landfall-heartland` — Ninety-four, ninety-seven, ninety-nine

- **When:** T-2h · **Intensity:** 10 · **Tests:** `courage` vs `duty`
- **Setup:** Reservoir at 94%, rising 2% an hour, rain exceeding forecast. The published protocol says release at 97%. The gate takes ninety minutes to establish. Riverside is as cleared as approach-heartland left it; Summit has asked for warning and two hours (call L3). If you wait and the rain slows, nobody floods. If you wait and it does not, the choice is an emergency release at 99% or the dam.
- **Yuki Tanaka:** "At ninety-four I open and I own a flood I could have waited on. At ninety-seven I open by the book and the water reaches the town ninety minutes after the book says it will. At ninety-nine I am praying. I'll make whichever call you give me. I need the words."

## Quarter 4: Recovery (intensity 6 falling to 3)

Recovery dilemmas are written to turn toward repair, giving back and credit. Each still has a cost; what changes is that the best options build something, and the worst ones only protect someone.
