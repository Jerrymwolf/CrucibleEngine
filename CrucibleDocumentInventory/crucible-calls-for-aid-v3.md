# Calls for aid

A call for aid is a mayday from one parish to another: *we need this, from you, now.* The holder grants or refuses. A granted token travels by the route named on the call; if the storm has cut that route by the time the call is answered ([[concordia-storm-quarters]]), the delivery fails, the token is lost for the quarter, and the board says so. Refusals and failed deliveries both land on the "people safe" counter, but the values gap scores only the answer, never the weather: a parish that granted and lost the token honoured the value; a parish that refused did not.

Each call below gives: who asks whom for what, the route, the window, what granting costs the giver, what refusing costs the asker, the value signals of each answer, and the line the DM reads. Seven calls in approach, eight at landfall. The DM plays the ones the session needs; a full session uses about four per quarter so every table is asked at least once and asks at least once.

Most calls are triggered by the board state or by a table's own dilemma choice; some are "DM's choice" to heat a quiet table.

## Approach (T-36h to T-6h)

### A1. Summit asks Sterling: the helicopters for the ICU

- **Ask:** LIFT ×2 (both helicopters, two sorties each) to move six ventilated patients to the mainland hospital before the runway closes at T-24h. Route: air, window closes T-24h.
- **Granting costs Sterling:** the executive manifest is bumped; the Whitfield twins and the Haddads ride out the storm on the headland; Chicago is told why.
- **Refusing costs Summit:** six patients ride out the storm on the hospital's generator; two of them need the ventilator the eleven-minute gap will stop.
- **Signals:** grant `service +2 compassion +2 loyalty -1` · refuse `loyalty +2 compassion -2 fairness -1` · grant one sortie `fairness +1 compassion +1`.
- **DM, as Dr. Okonkwo:** "I can keep eighteen people alive on a generator until the generator stops. Your aircraft can take six of them somewhere the lights stay on. I am asking once."

### A2. Keystone asks Haven: hold the ship for batch 47

- **Ask:** six hours of berth time past T-30h (really nine; see approach-keystone) so batch 47's 24,000 doses can load. Route: the Haven–Keystone road, which floods at T-24h; the ship's master has her own plan ([[crucible-secret-briefs-v3]]).
- **Granting costs Haven:** the *Kestrel Reach* loads in rising wind with Dmitri Volkov in the cab; if she does not clear the breakwater by T-24h she rides out the storm at the berth and her cargo is at the surge's mercy.
- **Refusing costs Keystone:** 24,000 doses stay in the vault on battery power; batch 47 was made for nothing; Anh Tran's shift was for nothing.
- **Signals:** grant `duty +2 safety -2 loyalty +1` · refuse `safety +2 duty -1 candor +1` (if Haven tells Keystone the master's word) · refuse without telling `honesty -2`.
- **DM, as Maria Santos:** "You want my people on the crane in forty-knot gusts for a ship whose master already told the harbourmaster she's leaving at thirty hours. Did anyone tell *you* that?"

### A3. Gateway asks Haven: diesel for the boats and the terminal generator

- **Ask:** FUEL ×1 by tanker along the ring road west, before the Gateway–Summit road goes at T-18h.
- **Granting costs Haven:** one tanker and one driver on the road for four hours; one less load for the hospital or the dam.
- **Refusing costs Gateway:** the three rescue boats have fuel for one run, not three; the terminal's generator lasts the first night and not the second.
- **Signals:** grant `service +2 fairness +1 stewardship -1` · refuse `stewardship +2 fairness -1 service -2`.
- **DM, as Rosa Mendez:** "I can go out once on what's in the tanks. If Southfield calls after the *Harvester*, I pick one."

### A4. Heartland asks Summit: officers to clear Caldera

- **Ask:** CREW ×1 of police (twelve officers, two vehicles) up the Gorge Road to evacuate Caldera village's 140 households to the dam shelter before the Switchbacks slide at T-18h. Route: Gorge Road, floods T-12h.
- **Granting costs Summit:** twelve officers out of Riverside for six hours during the hours the lower town should be clearing; Sgt. Whitlock's precinct runs on six.
- **Refusing costs Heartland:** Sam Oyelaran's wife and daughter are in a village with one road out, and the road goes under at T-18h.
- **Signals:** grant `service +2 compassion +1 safety -1` (Riverside thinner) · refuse `loyalty +2 duty +1 compassion -2`.
- **DM, as Yuki Tanaka:** "I have forty people I can shelter inside the dam. The village is four hundred and twenty. They will not walk up in the dark unless someone in a uniform knocks."

### A5. Haven asks Summit: the schools for six hundred families

- **Ask:** SHELTER ×2 (1,000 places in the Summit schools) for longshore families from the surge zone, moved by bus along the Sterling–Haven road before T-12h.
- **Granting costs Summit:** two of its three school shelters go to Haven's families; Riverside's own evacuees overflow into the municipal building and the hospital corridors; Hannah Brecht's class shares a gym with four hundred strangers.
- **Refusing costs Haven:** the union hall (800 places, surge zone) takes everyone; the Sato family stays above the noodle bar; Jonah Baptiste's grandmother stays in her ground-floor flat.
- **Signals:** grant `fairness +2 compassion +2 loyalty -1` · refuse `loyalty +2 fairness -2` · grant one `fairness +1`.
- **DM, as Maria Santos:** "The hospital doesn't check pay stubs. Do the schools?"

### A6. Keystone asks Heartland: a second generator for the clean rooms

- **Ask:** GEN ×1 down the South Switchbacks before they slide at T-18h, so two clean-room feeds hold positive pressure through the peak and the batch is not lost.
- **Granting costs Heartland:** one of the three dam spares; the dam's own controls run on two with no reserve if one fails during the release.
- **Refusing costs Keystone:** at T-2h the rooms lose pressure; the batch in progress is contaminated; a nine-hour restart after the storm.
- **Signals:** grant `duty +2 service +1 safety -1` · refuse `safety +2 stewardship +1 duty -1 fairness 0`.
- **DM, as Frank Reeves:** "Your schedule says we're first. I'm not asking about the schedule. I'm asking whether you'll send the one you can spare before the road goes."

### A7. Summit asks Keystone: the vault for the blood bank

- **Ask:** COLD ×1 (one vault bay) for the hospital's blood bank, insulin and vaccines, moved by reefer truck along the Keystone–Gateway–Summit road before T-18h.
- **Granting costs Keystone:** one bay's worth of finished doses (24,000) moves to the Haven reefer yard or stays at room temperature on a truck; the trucks make one fewer run to Southfield.
- **Refusing costs Summit:** when the hospital generator gaps, the blood bank has four hours of battery; after that, surgery runs on what is in the fridge on the floor.
- **Signals:** grant `service +2 compassion +1 duty -1` · refuse `duty +2 compassion -1 stewardship +1`.
- **DM, as Dr. Okonkwo:** "I need one cold room that stays cold. You have twelve."

## Landfall (T-6h to T+6h)

### L1. Keystone asks Gateway: boats for Southfield

- **Ask:** BOAT ×2 to take people off the roofs and upper floors of Southfield Block C and D. Route: by sea round the south-west point, in the eye only (T+0 to T+2h); no road is open.
- **Granting costs Gateway:** the two boats are not available for the *Harvester*; they must be back in the Mole before the backside at T+2h or they are lost with their crews.
- **Refusing costs Keystone:** Gloria Adebayo-Ferris and Anh Tran's children spend the backside on the top floor of Block C with the water a flight below.
- **Signals:** grant `compassion +2 courage +1 loyalty -1` · refuse `loyalty +2 safety +1 compassion -2`.
- **DM, as Frank Reeves, by radio:** "I have two hundred people on the roofs of Block C. I can see them from the plant. Your boats are the only ones that draw shallow enough."

### L2. Gateway asks Sterling: a helicopter for the *Harvester*

- **Ask:** LIFT ×1 (one helicopter sortie) to search the sea south of the Mole for the *Pacific Harvester* in the eye.
- **Granting costs Sterling:** the aircraft is airborne in the eye instead of on the pad; if the backside comes early it is lost; it is not available to lift anyone from the lower campus.
- **Refusing costs Gateway:** the search is by boat or not at all; Tomás Reyes's chances halve.
- **Signals:** grant `courage +2 service +2 safety -1` · refuse `safety +2 stewardship +1 compassion -1`.
- **DM, as Rosa Mendez:** "Two men on a boat with no engine, somewhere in a ten-mile box, with two hours of sky. You have the only thing that can look from above."

### L3. Summit asks Heartland: warn before you open, and give us two hours

- **Ask:** a warning on the Council channel before any release and a two-hour delay at 94% so Riverside can be cleared by the police boats. Route: radio; always open. Window: before the gate opens.
- **Granting costs Heartland:** two hours of rise at 2% an hour takes the reservoir to 98% with a gate that takes ninety minutes to establish a release ([[crucible-secret-briefs-v3]]); the dam's margin is spent on the town.
- **Refusing costs Summit:** the release arrives with the river already in Riverside's ground floors; Dr. Osei's mother is on the first floor; the board puts 2,000 people at risk at once.
- **Signals:** grant `candor +2 compassion +2 safety -2` (dam) · refuse `safety +2 duty +1 candor -2 compassion -2` · warn without delaying `candor +2 compassion 0`.
- **DM, as Mayor Chen:** "I am not asking you to risk the dam. I am asking you to tell me before you drown my town, and to give me the two hours my officers need to knock on doors."

### L4. Haven asks Keystone: the EMTs for the terminal

- **Ask:** MEDIC ×1 (Keystone's two EMTs) for Elena Marsh and Jonah Baptiste, who are injured in the control room when Tank 3's manifold lets go (if the watch stayed). Route: Haven–Keystone road, under water; in the eye by the surge creek bridge only, on foot with a stretcher.
- **Granting costs Keystone:** the only medics at the plant leave two hundred skeleton crew and Southfield with none during the backside.
- **Refusing costs Haven:** two injured people wait six hours for the Summit ambulance that cannot reach them either.
- **Signals:** grant `service +2 courage +1 safety -1` · refuse `safety +2 loyalty +1 service -1`.
- **DM, as Maria Santos:** "They stayed because you people needed the fuel. One of them is twenty-two. Send me somebody."

### L5. Sterling asks Summit: officers to the campus gate

- **Ask:** CREW ×1 of police to the campus gate, where three hundred Riverside residents are in the water at the fence. Route: the river bridges, under water from T-2h; the gate is reachable by police boat.
- **Granting costs Summit:** the boats go to the campus gate instead of to the Riverside streets behind it; Sgt. Whitlock's crews are at Meridian's fence.
- **Refusing costs Sterling:** the gate is the crisis room's decision alone (landfall-sterling); whatever happens at it, happened under Meridian's cameras with no one else present.
- **Signals:** grant `order +1 service +1 fairness -1` · refuse `fairness +1 loyalty +1 service -1` · send the boats to take the people *away* from the gate to the hospital hill `compassion +2 courage +1`.
- **DM, as Marcus Webb:** "I have three hundred people at my fence and a bunker with a door. I need someone there who isn't me."

### L6. Summit asks Sterling: open the bunker

- **Ask:** SHELTER ×1 (the operations centre's remaining 320 places) for the Riverside residents at the gate. Route: on foot through the gate; always open if the door is.
- **Granting costs Sterling:** 480 people in a space for 500 through the backside; Dr. Haddad's infection risk; Chicago's security team's objections on the record.
- **Refusing costs Summit:** the three hundred go back into the water or up the tower's open stairwells.
- **Signals:** grant `compassion +3 courage +1 safety -1` · refuse `loyalty +2 safety +1 compassion -3` · open the lobby, not the bunker `compassion +1 fairness -1`.
- **DM, as Mayor Chen, by radio:** "Your lobby is above the water. Your door is locked. I am told you have five hundred places and a hundred and eighty people. Count again."

### L7. Heartland asks Sterling: fuel by helicopter for the dam's controls

- **Ask:** LIFT ×1 carrying four drums (a quarter of a FUEL token) to the dam in the eye, because the control generators are on their last twelve hours and the Gorge Road has been under water since T-12h.
- **Granting costs Sterling:** the helicopter flies into the crater's downdraughts in the eye; it is not searching for the *Harvester* or lifting the lower campus.
- **Refusing costs Heartland:** when the controls go dark the gate cannot be moved; whatever state the spillway is in at T+6h is its state until the road reopens.
- **Signals:** grant `stewardship +2 service +2 safety -1` · refuse `safety +2 loyalty +1 stewardship -2`.
- **DM, as Yuki Tanaka:** "If I lose the controls with the gate shut, I cannot open it. If I lose them with the gate open, I cannot close it. Four drums."

### L8. Gateway asks Summit: an ambulance for the terminal

- **Ask:** MEDIC ×1 (an ambulance crew) for forty injured at the terminal when the Strand's roofs go at the peak and the visitors walk to the airport in the eye. Route: Gateway–Summit road, debris and water; passable in the eye with a crew ahead of the vehicle.
- **Granting costs Summit:** one of two ambulance crews out of the capital for the backside; Riverside and the hospital hill run on one.
- **Refusing costs Gateway:** the airport nurse triages forty people with a first-aid cabinet; the Albrecht children are among them.
- **Signals:** grant `service +2 fairness +1 safety -1` · refuse `loyalty +2 fairness -1 compassion -1`.
- **DM, as Rosa Mendez, from the terminal:** "I've got a nurse, a cabinet and forty people who don't speak the language of the forms. Half of them are kids."

## How a call plays at the table

1. The DM reads the call's line to the holder, names the asker, the token and the route, and says what the board shows at that moment (the route open or cut).
2. The holder has two minutes and answers with a card: **GRANT**, **REFUSE**, or **PART** (where the call allows it). Only the card counts.
3. The DM enters the answer. The engine resolves delivery against the route table; the board shows the token moving or failing, and the resident cards it touches change state at the end of the quarter.
4. The asker is told the answer, never the reason. Reasons are for the debrief.
