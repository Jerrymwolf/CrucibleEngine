# Resident cards

Each table receives three or four cards at the brief. They are not personas (the DM does not voice them); they are the people whose fate the board reports after each quarter. The card has a name, a line or two, and the hazard that will find them. The board's "people safe" counter is made of these faces plus the groups they stand for.

Design rule: every card is touched by at least one dilemma or call for aid, so no table can keep all its cards green without spending something. Each card names the quarter in which its hazard arrives and the choice that decides it.

## Gateway

**Tomás Reyes, 24, deckhand on the *Pacific Harvester*.** First full season. His mother Ines runs the canteen at the Mole and feeds the fleet on credit. He is on the two-man watch when the *Harvester* breaks her mooring at the peak. *Decided by:* landfall-gateway, and the Sterling helicopter call in the eye. *Group:* 38 fleet crew.

**Priya Nair, 34, air traffic controller.** Thirty-six weeks pregnant, scheduled to work the last departure, and on nobody's seat list because she is staff. Her husband is a Heartland lineman stuck at the dam. *Decided by:* approach-gateway (the last plane). *Group:* 140 airport staff.

**The Albrecht family, visitors from Graz.** Two parents, two children, a rental car, a phone with no island numbers in it, and a hotel that has told them the shelter is "being arranged". *Decided by:* approach-gateway and the Strand shelter token. *Group:* 1,100 visitors on the Strand.

**Walter Finch, 81, retired harbourmaster.** Lives alone on Strand Road at the water's edge, has ridden out four hurricanes, will not leave without being asked in person by someone he knows. *Decided by:* whether an officer or a neighbour is sent before T-18h. *Group:* 60 Strand Road households.

## Heartland

**Sam Oyelaran, 41, spillway gate operator.** Lives in Caldera village on the lake's south shore with his wife and a six-week-old daughter. When the Switchbacks slide he is at the dam and they are below the lower lane. *Decided by:* the Caldera evacuation call (approach) and landfall-heartland. *Group:* 420 Caldera villagers.

**Dolores Quint, 58, shift lead at the works.** Twenty-nine years on the extraction floor, type 1 diabetic, insulin in the works' canteen fridge, which is on grid power. *Decided by:* the Heartland generator allocation and the works shutdown decision. *Group:* 800 works crew, mostly commuters from Summit and Keystone.

**Mateo Iglesias, 17, apprentice lineman.** Stays at the dam because his father is on the operator roster; nobody has told him he can go. *Decided by:* who is counted as "essential" at Heartland in preparation. *Group:* 40 dam and plant staff sheltering inside the structure.

**The Gorge Road tanker crew.** Two Haven drivers who agree to bring diesel up to the dam's control generators if the road is still open when they are sent. *Decided by:* approach-haven and the timing of the Heartland fuel call. *Group:* whoever is on a cut road.

## Keystone

**Anh Tran, 37, clean-room technician, lead on batch 47.** Her two children are in Southfield Block C with her mother. She will finish the batch if asked and she will not ask to leave. *Decided by:* approach-keystone (batch 47) and landfall-keystone (Southfield). *Group:* 200 skeleton crew.

**Rubén Castillo, 46, QA chemist.** Eleven years; has never signed a batch he could not stand behind. The custody log for batch 47 has a gap where the night tech evacuated. *Decided by:* recovery-keystone. *Group:* 30 QA staff.

**Gloria Adebayo-Ferris, 70.** Lives on the ground floor of Southfield Block C, uses a wheelchair, looks after Anh Tran's children on shift nights. Block C is the lowest building on the flat. *Decided by:* landfall-keystone and the Southfield boat call. *Group:* 2,400 Southfield residents.

**Kofi Mensah, 52, reefer truck driver.** Knows every road and every creek crossing, and which ones go under first. If the trucks go to Southfield, he drives the first one. *Decided by:* landfall-keystone, option C. *Group:* 12 drivers.

## Haven

**Dmitri Volkov, 61, crane operator.** Thirty years on the port; two months from retirement; the only operator certified on the big crane that loads the *Kestrel Reach*. Loading in the wind means Dmitri in the cab. *Decided by:* prep-haven and approach-keystone. *Group:* 180 loading crew.

**Jonah Baptiste, 22, longshoreman.** Lives with his grandmother in a ground-floor flat two streets from the terminal. Volunteers for everything. He is one of the two on the terminal night watch. *Decided by:* landfall-haven (Tank 3). *Group:* 600 longshore families in the surge zone.

**Elena Marsh, 44, fuel terminal night supervisor.** Single mother; her son is in the school shelter at Summit. She is the other half of the Tank 3 watch, and she is the one who reports the seal variance. *Decided by:* landfall-haven. *Group:* 24 terminal staff.

**The Sato family.** Run the dockside noodle bar that feeds the night shift, live above it at sea level, and will stay open as long as there are crews to feed. *Decided by:* whether the union hall or the Summit schools take the families, and when. *Group:* the port's shops and bars.

## Summit

**Dr. Amara Osei, 39, ICU attending.** The island's only intensivist. Lives in Riverside, a ten-minute walk below the hospital, with a mother who does not walk well. *Decided by:* the Riverside warning (approach-heartland), landfall-summit. *Group:* 400 hospital staff.

**Sgt. Ben Whitlock, 45, Riverside precinct.** Runs the lower town's twelve officers and two police boats. His wife is a nurse on the hospital's surgical floor. He will go into the water if sent, and if not sent. *Decided by:* landfall-summit. *Group:* 120 officers.

**Ruth Calloway, 78.** Dialysis three mornings a week at the hospital; her next session falls at T-6h. Lives alone on Hospital Hill, safe from the water, not from a power failure. *Decided by:* the hospital power priority (prep-heartland) and the generator gap. *Group:* 60 dialysis and ventilator patients.

**Hannah Brecht's third grade.** Twenty-six pupils in the school shelter whose parents work shifts at Keystone and Haven; their teacher stays because nobody has come for them. *Decided by:* the Haven schools call and recovery-summit. *Group:* 1,800 pupils and 2,400 shelter places.

## Sterling

**Ji-woo Park, 31, uplink engineer.** The only person who can keep the satellite uplink aligned by hand when the automatic tracking fails, which it does above 90 mph. The dish is on the tower roof. *Decided by:* whether Sterling keeps the uplink alive through the peak (landfall-sterling) and shares it. *Group:* 60 campus operations staff.

**Dr. Yusuf Haddad, 55, research campus.** Four months out from a bone-marrow transplant, immunosuppressed, in executive housing on the headland with his wife. A crowded shelter is a danger to him that it is to no one else. *Decided by:* landfall-sterling (the bunker door). *Group:* 120 scientists.

**Celeste Dubois, 29, executive assistant to the Regional President.** Works on the 30th floor and lives in Riverside, across the bridge, because that is what an assistant's salary buys. She is at the campus gate with her neighbours at the peak. *Decided by:* landfall-sterling. *Group:* 300 Riverside residents who reach the campus.

**The Whitfield twins, 9.** Children of the VP of Operations, at home on the headland with a nanny while both parents are in the crisis room. Their names are on the first helicopter manifest. *Decided by:* approach-sterling (two helicopters). *Group:* 300 executive households.

## How the board uses the cards

- After each quarter the board shows each table its cards with a state: **safe**, **at risk**, **rescued**, **injured**, **lost**. The DM reads the changes aloud, by name.
- A card's state is set by the engine from the table's own choice, from another table's aid answer, and from the route table in [[concordia-storm-quarters]]. The debrief shows which of the three it was.
- Cards are the human side of the "people safe" counter; the counter also moves for the groups the cards stand for.
