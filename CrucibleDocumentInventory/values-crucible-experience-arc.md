# Values Crucible: Full Experience Arc

## The Learning Model

**The gap is the lesson.**

| Phase | Function | What's Captured |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Pre-Crisis | Establish baseline | Stated values, ranked priorities, commitments |
| During-Crisis | Create pressure | Decisions, rationales, behaviors under stress |
| Post-Crisis | Surface gap | Comparison of stated vs. enacted values |

Without pre-crisis values work, there's nothing to compare against.
Without during-crisis data capture, there's nothing to analyze.
Without post-crisis reflection, there's no transfer to real life.

---

## PRE-CRISIS: Grounding + Discovery (2 hours)

### 1. Personal Grounding (45 min)

**Purpose:** Individual reflection before group work. Creates psychological safety and personal stakes.

**Facilitator-led, low-tech:**

| Activity | Duration | Output |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Resonant leadership framing | 15 min | Shared language (attunement, dissonance, sacrifice syndrome) |
| Personal values card sort | 15 min | Individual top 5 values (private) |
| Reflection prompt | 10 min | Written: "A time I compromised a value under pressure" |
| Intention setting | 5 min | Written: "What I want to practice today" |

**What system captures:**
- Nothing yet. This is human-to-self work.
- Facilitator may collect reflection cards for later (optional).

---

### 2. Team Formation (15 min)

**Purpose:** Assign teams to parishes, establish team identity.

| Activity | Duration | Output |
|----------|----------|--------|
| Parish assignment | 5 min | Team → Parish mapping |
| Team introductions | 5 min | Names, roles in real organization |
| Parish briefing | 5 min | Read parish context card |

**What system captures:**
- Team ID → Parish ID mapping
- Participant names and real-world roles

---

### 3. Team Values Discovery (45 min)

**Purpose:** Teams surface, negotiate, and commit to shared values. This creates the "stated values" baseline.

| Activity | Duration | Platform |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Individual sharing | 10 min | In-person discussion |
| Values nomination | 10 min | Digital: each member submits 3-5 values |
| Convergence discussion | 15 min | In-person with digital display |
| Ranking and commitment | 10 min | Digital: team ranks top 5, writes commitment |

**The Deception Layer:**

Some metrics are:
- **Accurate** — Real consequences of decisions
- **Delayed** — Show old data during communication disruptions
- **Optimistic** — Corporate reporting that downplays problems
- **Manipulated** — Pressure from headquarters to show better numbers

Teams don't know which is which. This mirrors real corporate information environments.

**CM Control:** Crucible Master can:
- Inject false metrics ("Singapore says they received contaminated batch")
- Reveal hidden truth ("Actual worker injuries not reported to corporate")
- Create information conflicts ("Haven reports 12 boats out; Gateway says 8")

### Values Check-Ins (CM-Initiated)

At key moments, CM can trigger structured reflection mid-crisis:

**Flash Check (30 seconds):**
> "Before you decide: Which of your five values is guiding this choice? Name it out loud."

**Pause Check (2 minutes):**
> "Stop. Look at your commitment statement. Read it aloud. Now continue."

**Conflict Check (when team disagrees):**
> "You're split. Each side: What value are you protecting? Is this a values conflict or a tactics conflict?"

**What system captures:**
- Check-in timestamp
- Which value(s) team names
- Decision made immediately after

### Decision Capture (Every Choice)

For every team decision, system records:

```typescript
interface DecisionRecord {
  // Identity
  id: string;
  teamId: string;
  parishId: string;
  timestamp: number;
  simulationTime: number;
  
  // The choice
  categoryId: string;
  decisionTypeId: string;
  choice: string;
  rationale: string;  // Team's stated reason
  
  // Context
  intensityLevel: number;
  activeCrises: string[];
  recentEvents: string[];  // What just happened
  
  // Values analysis (from ValuesObserver agent)
  statedValuesAtDecision: string[];  // Their ranked values
  valuesAlignment: {
    value: string;
    score: -3 to +3;
    evidence: string;
  }[];
  abcd: {
    affect: string;    // Emotional state
    behavior: string;  // Observable actions
    cognition: string; // Reasoning expressed
    desire: string;    // Outcome sought
  };
  
  // Flags
  notableMoment: boolean;
  notableReason?: string;
  valuesConflictDetected: boolean;
  commitmentStatementViolation: boolean;
}
```

### Consequence Transparency Levels

Not all consequences are immediately visible:

| Level | Visibility | Example |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Immediate | Team sees instantly | "Generator deployed to hospital" |
| Delayed | Team sees after time passes | "Worker who stayed late injured driving home" |
| Hidden | Only CM sees | "Morale dropping in workforce" |
| Revealed | Surfaces in recovery phase | "Three employees quit citing safety concerns" |
| Long-term | Discussed in debrief | "Reputation damage affects recruiting for years" |

### 4. The Rationalization Cascade (15 min)

**Facilitator presents the pattern:**

```
THE DRIFT

1. "Storm probably won't hit"
   → Keep production running
   
2. "Employees need the income"
   → Justify not sending people home early
   
3. "We're serving cancer patients globally"
   → Production becomes morally righteous
   
4. "Local government should handle local issues"
   → Abdicate community responsibility
   
5. "Shareholders have rights too"
   → Fiduciary duty as moral shield
   
6. "We'll help with recovery after"
   → Defer responsibility to future self

Each step feels reasonable.
The destination is indefensible.
```

**Discussion:**
- "Where did you see yourself on this cascade?"
- "Which rationalization was most seductive?"
- "What could have interrupted the drift?"

---

### 5. Transfer to Real Context (20 min)

**Purpose:** Bridge from simulation to actual work life.

**Individual reflection (5 min):**
> "Think of a real situation in your organization where you've felt pressure similar to what you experienced today. Write briefly:
> - What was the pressure?
> - Which value was tested?
> - What did you do?
> - What would you do differently now?"

**Pair share (5 min):**
- Share with one person (not from your simulation team)
- Listen without advising

**Commitment (5 min):**
> "Complete this sentence and share with your pair:
> 'The next time I feel [specific pressure], I will [specific action] to stay aligned with [specific value].'"

**Whole group (5 min):**
- Facilitator invites 2-3 voluntary shares
- No commentary, just witness

---

### 6. Closing (5 min)

- Facilitator honors the work done
- Reminder: The gap is data, not judgment
- Resources for continued development
- Logistics for follow-up (reports emailed, etc.)
