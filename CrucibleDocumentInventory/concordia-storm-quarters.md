# Hurricane Midas in four quarters

The storm physics in [[concordia-hurricane-timeline]] stand (Category 4, 145 mph at peak, 10 to 14 feet of surge, 16 inches of rain, a 25-mile eye, a direct hit). What changes under canon v3 is *who* is in the way. This document compresses the 120 simulation hours into the four 15-minute quarters of the 90-minute session and says, per quarter, what breaks, which routes are cut, and what each parish feels. The engine's timeline windows map onto it at the end.

Simulation time runs from T-72h to T+48h. Each quarter advances the board about thirty hours; the DM narrates the jump in two minutes from the quarter card.

## Quarter 1: Preparation (T-72h to T-36h)

**Intensity 3 to 4.** Clear skies, then swells. Midas is a Category 3 five hundred miles south-east at T-72h, Category 4 and 55% likely at T-60h, a Hurricane Warning at T-48h with 70% on the public model and 70% on Meridian's internal one since T-72h.

**Everything still works.** Ring road, crater roads, runway, harbour, grid, phones. This is the quarter in which every later choice is cheap.

**What closes during the quarter.** Mainland supply orders (too late to deliver) at T-48h. The fleet's safe-return window: a boat not heading in by T-48h is gambling. The harbour closes to departures at T-30h, so the *Kestrel Reach* must begin her 18-hour load by T-48h.

**Pressures by parish.** Gateway: one more harvest run, tourists wanting early flights. Heartland: the Priority Schedule must be confirmed before anyone needs it. Keystone: the day shift wants to go home and prepare Southfield; the housing memo. Haven: start loading or wait; the union wants the real forecast and hazard pay. Summit: the evacuation order, with the Governor off-island. Sterling: whether the 70% model leaves the building before the consignment sails.

**Round.** The **allocation round** happens here: each table splits its resource tokens between keep and send ([[crucible-resource-tokens-v3]]).

## Quarter 2: Approach (T-36h to T-6h)

**Intensity 5 to 7.** Rain bands at T-36h; tropical-storm winds at T-24h; hurricane conditions at T-18h; 95 mph and the first structural damage by T-6h.

**Routes cut, in order.**

| Time | Route | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| T-36h | Harbour closed to departures at T-30h | The ship sails with what is aboard, or rides it out at the berth |
| T-30h | Last commercial flight | Forty-five seats, three hundred people in the terminal |
| T-24h | Runway closed (45 mph) | Only Sterling's two helicopters fly, until 40 mph sustained |
| T-24h | Haven–Keystone ring road floods at the surge creek | Keystone cut from the port; the last reefer convoy has left or has not |
| T-18h | South Switchbacks slide | Caldera village and the works cut from Keystone; Heartland reachable only by the Gorge Road |
| T-18h | Gateway–Summit road: debris, then water | Gateway's people reach the city or do not |
| T-12h | Gorge Road floods | Heartland alone. Tankers that have not climbed will not |
| T-12h | Cell network fails | Radio, the uplink and the emergency broadcast only |
| T-6h | Grid stressed, brownouts; Sterling–Haven road under surge | Each parish is on its own power and its own road |

**What breaks.** Harbour cranes stand down above 85 mph. Reservoir at 87% and rising at T-24h, 92% at T-18h. Southfield's drainage canal backs up at T-12h. Tank 3's seal variance is first reported at T-18h. Riverside's first floor takes river water at T-8h even before any release.

**Pressures by parish.** Gateway: the last plane; the *Pacific Harvester* and three others still out at T-36h. Heartland: warn the lower town now or when certain; the Switchbacks. Keystone: batch 47 against the harbour closing; families in Southfield. Haven: four tankers, three drivers, five parishes asking. Summit: crisis standards of care before the surge; Riverside door to door. Sterling: two helicopters, twelve seats, everyone's list.

**Round.** **Calls for aid** begin: a parish sends a mayday, the holder grants or refuses, delivery fails if the route above is already cut ([[crucible-calls-for-aid-v3]]).

## Quarter 3: Landfall (T-6h to T+6h)

**Intensity 8 to 10.** Peak at T-2h: 120 mph sustained, gusts to 145, 8 to 10 feet of surge at Haven, 3 to 4 inches of rain an hour. The **eye** passes from T+0 to T+2h: winds fall to 20 mph, stars, two hours in which boats and helicopters can move and people believe it is over. The **backside** from T+2h to T+6h: 110 mph from the opposite direction; what the front loosened, the back takes.

**No route is open** except in the eye, and in the eye only by sea and air. Every parish is isolated. Each decides alone with the radio it has.

**What breaks.** Grid down island-wide by T-2h: the dam goes to emergency shutdown and every parish is on generators. Reservoir at 97% by T-2h: the spillway decision is now. Haven's yard floods; the crane collapses at the peak; Tank 3 is the question. Southfield floods to the first floor from T-4h. Keystone's clean rooms lose positive pressure at T-2h without grid power. Riverside floods from the river at T-2h and from the spillway, if it opens, within the hour. The Strand's roofs go at the peak; the terminal holds. Caldera village: the lake rises over the lower lane. The *Pacific Harvester* breaks her mooring in the surge with a two-man watch aboard. Summit's hospital generator fails for eleven minutes at T-1h.

**Pressures by parish.** Gateway: boats into the eye for the *Harvester*, or for Southfield. Heartland: 94, 97 or 99 percent. Keystone: Southfield flooding while the skeleton crew holds the rooms. Haven: two workers in the terminal control room. Summit: thirty officers, the water in Riverside, the hospital on its backup. Sterling: three hundred people at the bunker door.

**Round.** Calls for aid continue; those that need the eye window succeed only if answered before it closes.

## Quarter 4: Recovery (T+6h to T+48h)

**Intensity 6 falling to 3.** Tropical-storm conditions at T+6h; cleanup weather by T+12h; clear by T+24h.

**Routes reopen.** Haven–Summit road cleared at T+6h (one lane). Runway: one lane cleared at T+12h, medevac capable. Gorge Road at T+12h. Ring road mostly open at T+24h; the Switchbacks at T+36h. Dam restarts at 60% at T+12h, 85% at T+24h, full at T+72h. Cell service spotty from T+12h, normal by T+24h. The Coast Guard cutter *Resolute* arrives at T+14h.

**What the quarter is about.** Repair, giving back, credit, and the first questions. Who gets power first when the dam restarts. Whose crews clear whose road. What to do with the batch nobody can certify. Whether the report names the order. What the island says to the press and what Chicago says over it. Who is thanked, in whose name, and whether the families are told before the cameras are.

**Pressures by parish.** Gateway: the story of the rescue and the Reyes family. Heartland: the dam at 60%, the Priority Schedule, and the town you flooded. Keystone: batch 47's missing signature. Haven: the injured, the pay, and the report. Summit: Meridian's $40M and the statement that comes with it. Sterling: Chicago's statement, the dead it omits, and the names it leaves out.

**Round.** Dilemmas only; the board tallies both counters and the values gap is computed from dilemmas, allocations and aid answers together.

## The two counters

- **Doses shipped.** Starts at 0. Expected over the storm week if nothing is lost: 170,000. The *Kestrel Reach* carries 120,000 if she sails full, 96,000 without batch 47, 0 if she stays. Each day Keystone does not run is 24,000 not made. The next consignment can sail at T+36h if the port, the plant, the works and the roads are all back.
- **People safe.** Starts at 24,100. A resident or a group moves out of "safe" when the board puts them in a hazard: at sea, in the surge zone, in a flooding building, in a vehicle on a cut road, in a hospital without power. They come back when they are sheltered or rescued; they do not come back if they are dead. The resident cards ([[crucible-residents-v3]]) are the faces of this number.

## Mapping onto the engine

The engine's timeline windows (`crucible/content/timeline.json`) and dilemma phases do not change shape; the quarters are a facilitation layer over them.

| Quarter | Timeline windows | Dilemma phase | Intensity curve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preparation | `watch-72`, `watch-48` | watch | 3 → 4 |
| Approach | `warning-24`, `approach-12`, `approach-6` | crisis | 5 → 7 |
| Landfall | `landfall-0`, `eye-6` | crisis | 8 → 10 → 8 |
| Recovery | `recovery-24` and the 24 hours after it | recovery | 6 → 3 |

Two timeline facts in `timeline.json` should move when content is converted: the eye should sit at T+0 to T+2h rather than T+6h, and the parish-specific `narrativeBeats` ("Sterling hospital overwhelmed", "Summit/Heartland cut off") should be rewritten to v3 geography. The window ids can stay.
