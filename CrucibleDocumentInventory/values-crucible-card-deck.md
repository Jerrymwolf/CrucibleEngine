## Values Organized by Schwartz Circumplex

The circular structure shows which values tend to cluster together (compatible) and which tend to conflict (opposite sides of the circle).

```
                    OPENNESS TO CHANGE
                    (Self-Direction, Creativity,
                     Curiosity, Independence)
                           │
                           │
    SELF-ENHANCEMENT ──────┼────── SELF-TRANSCENDENCE
    (Achievement,          │       (Benevolence, Service,
     Success, Impact,      │        Fairness, Justice,
     Recognition)          │        Compassion, Equality)
                           │
                           │
                    CONSERVATION
                    (Security, Stability,
                     Tradition, Loyalty,
                     Duty, Discipline)
```

**Compatibility:** Values adjacent on the circle tend to support each other.
**Conflict:** Values across the circle from each other often create tension.

**Example Tensions:**
- Achievement vs. Benevolence (my success vs. others' needs)
- Independence vs. Loyalty (my judgment vs. group commitment)
- Security vs. Creativity (predictability vs. innovation)

## Facilitator Notes: Common Patterns

### Most Frequently Selected (across populations)
1. Integrity
2. Honesty
3. Family/Relationships (if included)
4. Respect
5. Accountability

### Frequently Underselected (but critical in crisis)
1. Courage (easy to say, hard to live)
2. Candor (people prefer harmony)
3. Fairness (people prefer those close to them)
4. Safety (gets deprioritized under goal pressure)

### Coast Guard Specific Considerations
Based on your research, Coast Guard members often cluster around:
- Honor, Respect, Devotion to Duty (core values)
- Service, Duty, Loyalty (mission orientation)
- Courage, Resilience (operational necessity)

Watch for tension between:
- Duty to mission vs. Safety of crew
- Loyalty to unit vs. Honesty about problems
- Honor (idealized) vs. Accountability (actual)

## Customization for Client Organizations

When running Values Crucible for a specific organization, consider adding:
1. Their stated organizational values
2. Industry-specific values
3. Role-specific values (e.g., "Patient Care" for healthcare)

**Maximum deck size:** 50 cards (beyond that, decision fatigue sets in)

**Recommended customization:** Replace 5-8 general values with client-specific values while maintaining coverage across all Schwartz clusters.
