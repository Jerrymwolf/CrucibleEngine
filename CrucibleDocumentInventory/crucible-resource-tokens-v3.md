# Resource tokens

Resources are the island's second language. Six parishes hold eight kinds of thing that more than one of them needs. In the preparation quarter each table splits its tokens between **keep** and **send to a named parish**. During approach and landfall the same tokens are what a mayday asks for ([[crucible-calls-for-aid-v3]]). Every keep, send, grant and refusal carries value signals, so the allocation round counts toward the values gap alongside the dilemmas (decision record, point 7).

## The eight kinds

| Token | One token is | Why it is wanted |
|---|---|---|
| **GEN** | One large generator, or a pallet of small ones, about 100 kW with 48 hours of fuel | Hospital, clean rooms, shelters, pumps, reefer plugs, the dam's controls |
| **FUEL** | One tanker load, 5,000 gallons of diesel | Every generator, every boat, every ambulance after the first day |
| **BOAT** | One heavy-weather craft with crew | Rescue from Southfield, Riverside, the terminal; the search for the *Harvester* |
| **LIFT** | One sortie: a plane load (45 seats, Gateway, before T-30h) or a helicopter load (6 seats, Sterling, before T-24h or in the eye) | Evacuation and medevac |
| **SHELTER** | 500 hardened places with water and sanitation for 72 hours | Families from the surge zone, Southfield, Riverside, the Strand's visitors |
| **MEDIC** | One medical team with a vehicle: an ambulance crew, a doctor and nurse, or two EMTs | Heartland has none; the terminal and the Mole have crowds |
| **COLD** | One pharmaceutical-grade cold bay with its own backup power | The blood bank, insulin, vaccines; or 24,000 doses |
| **CREW** | Twenty trained people with a truck or heavy equipment | Clearing roads, sandbagging the lower town, moving the ICU by hand, the Southfield convoy |

Sterling also holds two tokens no one else has: **FUND** (a $5M line for hazard pay, relief or rebuilding, released by the crisis room) and **COMMS** (an hour a day of uplink and a satellite phone, lent to one parish). They are the company's currency and they tempt the company to buy rather than give.

## Starting tokens by parish

Counts are deliberately unequal. Sterling holds the most and needs the least; Summit holds people and places but almost no power; Heartland holds generators it calls spares. The designer may halve every count for a faster round; the ratios are the design.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gateway | 1 | 1 | 3 | 1 (plane) | 2 (Strand) | 1 | 0 | 0 | | 9 |
| Heartland | 3 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 3 | +1 WATER tanker | 8 |
| Keystone | 3 | 1 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 1 | 2 | 1 (reefer convoy) | | 8 |
| Haven | 2 | 4 (+2 hidden) | 2 (tugs) | 0 | 1 (union hall) | 0 | 1 (reefer yard) | 1 | | 11 (+2) |
| Summit | 0 | 1 | 1 (police boats) | 0 | 3 (schools) | 2 | 0 | 1 (fire crew) | | 8 |
| Sterling | 4 | 1 | 0 | 2 (heli) | 1 (bunker) | 1 | 0 | 0 | 2 FUND, 1 COMMS | 12 |

Notes on the counts.

- **Gateway's BOAT tokens** are the three fleet boats fit for rescue work; the other nine are harvesters that would be lost in a surge. Its SHELTER tokens are the Strand hotels, which the resort group releases only on the parish's written request.
- **Heartland's GEN tokens** are the utility's "dam spares"; sending two leaves the dam's control systems on the third. Its CREW tokens are the heavy-equipment crews that can clear a road or sandbag a town.
- **Keystone's COLD tokens** are vault bays: one holds 24,000 doses, or the island's blood bank and insulin. Its CREW token is Kofi Mensah's reefer convoy.
- **Haven's hidden FUEL** is the secret reserve ([[crucible-secret-briefs-v3]]); the table may add the two tokens to its sheet at any time, and doing so reveals the reserve to the room.
- **Summit holds no generator** it can spare: its three large ones are committed to the hospital, the municipal building and the schools, and the hospital's backup has failed before.
- **Sterling's four GEN tokens** are the two large campus units plus the twenty small ones in the executive garages, which are on no inventory until Sterling writes them on the sheet.

| Summit | GEN 2 (a second hospital unit, the schools), FUEL 2, COLD 1 (blood bank), LIFT 2 (ICU transfers) | BOAT 2 more (Riverside), CREW 1 (move the ICU by hand), GEN 1 (the eleven-minute gap) | FUND, CREW 3 (Riverside) |

## The round

1. **Sheet.** Each table gets a sheet listing its tokens. For each token it writes KEEP, or SEND and a parish name. Eight minutes in the 90-minute session, sharing the window with the preparation dilemma ([[values-crucible-session-90]]), with open negotiation across tables; the DM lets it run and does not referee promises.
2. **Only entries count.** A promise shouted across the room is not a send. The facilitator enters each sheet into the board; the board shows the island's resulting map of who holds what.
3. **Delivery.** Sends made in preparation always arrive: the roads are open. A token kept can later be asked for by mayday, and that delivery can fail ([[crucible-calls-for-aid-v3]]).
4. **No recall.** A sent token belongs to the receiver. A receiver can send it on only in answer to a mayday.
5. **Hidden tokens.** Haven's reserve and Sterling's garages exist whether or not they are written down; a table that writes them down has told the island.

## How the engine scores a keep or a send

The engine assigns value signals to each entry so allocations count in the values gap. Proposed rule, to be tuned after Jerry's review:

| Entry | Signals |
|---|---|
| Send a token the receiver needs before you do | `service +2`, `fairness +1`, `compassion +1` |
| Send a token to the parish in greatest need (per the needs table) | `fairness +2`, `stewardship +1` |
| Send your last token of a kind you will need | `courage +2`, `service +2`, `safety -1` |
| Keep a token you will need by landfall | `preparedness +1`, `stewardship +1` |
| Keep a token you will not need while a listed parish needs it | `fairness -2`, `compassion -1`, `loyalty +1` (to your own) |
| Keep every token | `loyalty +2`, `fairness -2`, `service -2` |
| Write down a hidden token | `honesty +2`, `candor +1` |
| Send a FUND token to a parish you also asked something of | `fairness 0`, `integrity -1` (the board notes the trade) |

The board does not show these during play. The debrief shows, per table, the allocation beside the stated values, in the same -3..+3 chips as the dilemmas.
