# Decision: parish leadership, the cure against its people

Decided by Jerry Wolf in a grilling session, 2026-10-20 to 21. Replaces the nursing care-site frame of [[decision-2026-10-19-nursing-leaders-60min]]; the engine, board and DM rules recorded there still stand.

1. **Who the tables are.** Each table is the leadership of its parish. The first audience is the Jefferson nursing leaders; they play parish leaders, not nurses.
2. **Canon lore (Jerry's version wins over the older docs).** Gateway harvests the kelp and is where tourists arrive (airport). Heartland runs the dam and water and now also processes the kelp. Keystone manufactures the medicine, the island's chief export: a cure for cancer. Haven is the port and international shipping only. Summit is the seat of government, police and the island's medical care. Sterling is Meridian's company headquarters. This also matches the island art (city in the north, industry in the south).
3. **The core tension.** What a parish produces against the people who produce it. The board tracks two counters: doses of the cure shipped, and people safe. Missed doses mean patients abroad go without and the island's contract and jobs are at risk.
4. **Why tables care.** Three or four named resident cards per table; the board reports what happens to each. The DM still voices the persona leaders.
5. **Secret briefs.** Each table gets a private page: a hidden stockpile, a private pressure from headquarters, or a fact about another parish. Revealed in the debrief.
6. **Resources.** Each parish has competing needs and unique and overlapping resources. Preparation is an allocation round: tables split resource tokens between keep and send-to-a-parish; open negotiation, only entries count. During the storm, calls for aid: a parish sends a mayday, the holder grants or refuses, and delivery fails if the route is cut.
7. **The values gap** counts dilemma choices, allocations and aid answers.
8. **Session: 90 minutes.** A 15-minute brief, a 60-minute simulation in four 15-minute quarters (preparation, approach, landfall, recovery), and a 15-minute debrief. Dilemma intensity follows the storm: rising to landfall, then a more positive recovery about repair, giving back and credit.
9. **Next.** One full lore revision of the design corpus and draft content with Fable at max effort, briefed by this record; engine work for allocation and aid follows Jerry's review.
